Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Autoposter Tiktok ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner with the owner's own TikTok account.

Data the app accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open id, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope.
  • Permission to upload videos as drafts via the video.upload scope.

How data is stored and used

  • Access tokens and the video queue are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, through the official API. Captions may be generated with the Anthropic Claude API from the video's title and notes.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored tokens.
